A toy kernel, not the APNT recipe

The useful community example is a tiny relation that proves knowledge of a committed secret and binds that authorization to one synthetic BCH transaction projection. It can teach the complete handoff without publishing Proofnote’s note assignment, Recovery, fee, batching, or aggregation design.
